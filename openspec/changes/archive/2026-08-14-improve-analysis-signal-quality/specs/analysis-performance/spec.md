## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Revised signal policy stays in the measured flow
Performance evidence SHALL measure role classification, recovery retention,
stable package construction, relation evidence, history filtering, version-3
construction, ranking, de-duplication, and streaming output in the same
correctness-checked invocation.

#### Scenario: A sample is timed
- **WHEN** public or private aggregate evidence is accepted
- **THEN** that invocation already passed semantics, schema, indexes, privacy, bytes, and exact work counts

### Requirement: Revised tables retain hot-path limits
Package, advisory, relation, and history storage SHALL reserve from known input
counts. Rating, graph aggregation, coupling threshold evaluation, and ranking
hot loops SHALL not allocate after prepared storage is available.

#### Scenario: Generated and recovered context is retained
- **WHEN** it is excluded from verdicts but kept for detail
- **THEN** allocation and peak-memory evidence remain inside reviewed flow budgets

### Requirement: Workload evidence records role and trust shape
Public workloads and all three reviewed workload families SHALL record workload
identity, reviewed revision, dirty state, toolchain, host, source bytes, role
counts, trust counts, supported files, wall time, p95, peak memory, allocations,
reads, and Git process counts. Changed identity SHALL require a new review.

#### Scenario: A role rule changes workload shape
- **WHEN** selected or verdict-affecting counts change
- **THEN** old performance evidence cannot be reused under the prior identity
